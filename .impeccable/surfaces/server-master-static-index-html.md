# Inference dashboard

Mode: Operate. A night-shift operator needs to identify a pedestrian, track elapsed dwell, and review the resulting warning and frames on an offline LAN.

THESIS: Put every device on one full-width status board and give its evidence a separate reading area.

OWN-WORLD: A copper and graphite screenprint of a pedestrian crossing sets the visual identity. Condensed lettering identifies sections and a quiet neutral grid carries the live data.

STORY: Check the connection, scan the ordered device rows, select a device to inspect its frames, then open a recorded warning snapshot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact navigation and pause control, illustrated title with clock and read-only config, then device status. The evidence area begins with frames and the warning log side by side on wide screens.

FORM: The user-supplied Sazabi reference informed the condensed typography, tonal image treatment and restrained dark chrome. The crossing artwork and console mark are original.

FINISH: Use labeled statuses, keyboard-accessible selections and dialogs, and reduced motion. Narrow layouts preserve scores and timestamps in labeled records.
